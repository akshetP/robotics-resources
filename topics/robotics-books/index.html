Books to learn robotics

Books remain useful for systematic coverage of ROS concepts, tooling and examples. This collection focuses on titles that learners can access online while working through tutorials.

6 curated links · Updated 2026-07-29

Direct answer

What belongs in this collection?

Use a book alongside a simulator and a tutorial playlist. Do not wait until you have read everything before writing your first node.

How to use these resources

  1. Skim the list and open two or three links that match your current level.
  2. Prefer resources with active documentation or recent commits when you need production guidance.
  3. Combine one tutorial source with one simulator and one small project rather than collecting endless bookmarks.
