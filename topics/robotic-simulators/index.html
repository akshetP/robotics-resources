Robotic simulators

Robotic simulators let you test algorithms, robot models and factory workflows safely without physical hardware. Options range from research engines such as MuJoCo and Gazebo to educational platforms, ROS-integrated tools, industrial digital twins and high-fidelity game-engine workflows for different budgets and skill levels.

21 curated links · Updated 2026-07-29

Direct answer

What belongs in this collection?

Beginners often start with Gazebo, Webots or the simulator used by their chosen course because documentation matters more than raw feature count. Specialists may later need Isaac Sim, RoboDK or vendor-specific environments for perception, manufacturing or digital-twin workflows and project constraints.
