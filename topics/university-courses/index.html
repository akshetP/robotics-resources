Free university courses in robotics

Free university courses provide structured robotics learning with lecture notes, assignments or video series from recognised institutions. They are one of the highest-leverage ways to build foundations without paying for a full degree module.

8 curated links · Updated 2026-07-29

Direct answer

What belongs in this collection?

Pick one course and finish it before collecting more bookmarks. Complement lectures with simulator practice and a small open-source project.

Curated links

  • Self-Driving Cars with Duckietown
  • Introduction To Robotics by Massachusetts Institute of Technology
  • QUT Robot Academy by QUT (Queensland University of Technology)
  • Introduction to Robotics by Stanford University
  • Introduction to Mobile Robotics by The University of Freiburg
  • Applied robot design by Stanford University
  • Underactuated Robotics by MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL)
  • Advanced Robotics by University of California, Berkeley

How to use these resources

  1. Skim the list and open two or three links that match your current level.
  2. Prefer resources with active documentation or recent commits when you need production guidance.
  3. Combine one tutorial source with one simulator and one small project rather than collecting endless bookmarks.
